Work requirements

Unless you're exempt, do ONE of these for at least 1 month out of every 6:

  • Option 1

    Earn $580+ in the month

    $580: seasonal workers can average it over 6 months.

  • Option 2

    Go to school half-time

    School: high school, GED, college, or trade school (at least half-time).

  • Option 3

    Do 80 hours of activities

    80 hours a month: any combination of work, volunteering, school, or a work program.

Sources: HFS work rules toolkit, HFS letter to ACA Adults

New applicants

You must have met one requirement in the month before you apply. Keep proof. HFS can ask for proof from any month since your last renewal.

Source: Get Medicaid Facts

Work rules are for ACA Adults. Does this apply to you?

You don't need to meet work requirements if you:

Check the list. If one is true for you, you may not need to meet the work rules. HFS makes the final decision.

  • Are younger than 19 or older than 64
  • Have Medicare or qualify for it
  • Are in another Medicaid group (AABD, Moms & Babies, Family Care)
  • Care for a child under 14 or a dependent person with a disability
  • Are a veteran rated 100% disabled
  • Are American Indian or Alaska Native
  • Have special medical needs, like a serious condition needing regular treatment, a serious mental health or substance use condition, or a disability
  • Get TANF and follow its work rules
  • Meet SNAP work rules, or are in SNAP's 3-month grace period
  • Are pregnant, or your pregnancy ended in the last 12 months
  • Were in foster care and are now under 26
  • Are in drug or alcohol treatment
  • Are in jail or prison, or got out in the last 3 months
  • Have a short-term hardship, like a hospital stay

Update: On Oct. 29, 2026, a federal judge ruled that people with special medical needs don't also have to prove they can't work. Illinois was one of the states that sued. HFS hasn't updated its guidance yet.

Not sure? Try the "Am I exempt?" quiz

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS Medicaid FAQs, Stateline: court ruling, Illinois Attorney General

Keeping your coverage

  • ACA Adults must renew every 6 months, even if exempt.
  • If HFS asks for more information, you have 30 days to send it. You're still covered during that time.
  • If you're not exempt AND you don't meet the rules, Medicaid stops when your coverage period ends.
  • Meeting SNAP's work rules counts for Medicaid. A SNAP exemption may not.
  • By Jan. 2027, you can ask for an exemption and send work proof in Manage My Case. If the State can't confirm you're exempt in 2027, you can promise that you are through a written attestation. (Lying about it is a crime.)

Sources: HFS exemptions guide, HFS letter to ACA Adults, Illinois HFS

Steps to take now

  1. Sign in to Manage My Case

    Go to abe.illinois.gov. Check your medical group and renewal date.

  2. Tell HFS about changes

    Tell HFS right away if your address, phone, email, or immigration status changes.

  3. Read State mail right away

    HFS mailed letters in Sept. 2026.

Sources: Illinois HFS, HFS work rules toolkit

Non-citizens

Since Oct. 1, 2026, non-citizens keep Medicaid only if they:

  • Have had a green card 5+ years, or have one and were a refugee, asylee, or trafficking survivor
  • Are a Cuban-Haitian entrant or COFA citizen (Micronesia, Marshall Islands, or Palau)
  • Are under 19 or pregnant (any immigration status)
  • Have a green card and are a veteran, on active duty, or their spouse or child
  • Were already in HBIS, AATV, or certain other state programs

Sources: HFS non-citizen notice, HFS non-citizen guide, Federal Medicaid guidance (CMS)

Who lost Medicaid

Refugees, asylees, parolees (including Ukrainian and Afghan), and trafficking/domestic violence survivors without a green card, among others. Getting a green card may restore Medicaid.

Lost Medicaid? You can still get care

Sources: HFS non-citizen toolkit, SHVS immigrant eligibility brief

If you lose Medicaid

Citizens and qualifying non-citizens can apply for marketplace insurance through Get Covered Illinois.

Many people can get help paying premiums, but NOT if you lost Medicaid for not meeting work rules.

If you're living with HIV, the AIDS Drug Assistance Program may help pay certain marketplace premiums. Call 1-800-825-3518. TTY: 1-800-547-0466

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS prescription coverage, AIDS Foundation of Chicago: premium help
