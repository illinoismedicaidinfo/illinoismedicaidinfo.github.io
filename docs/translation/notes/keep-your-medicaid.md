# Translator notes: es/keep-your-medicaid.html

First-draft translation, 23 Nov. 2026. Parity check passes. Every external link marked "Spanish URL" was swapped per `link-map.md`; all others keep the English URL and say "(en inglés)".

**Brief vs. page:** the brief said this page has the Manage My Case click path (Case Summary → Benefit Details → Notices). It does not. Step 1 only says to sign in and check the "medical group" and renewal date. The click path is on `index` and `am-i-exempt` (see glossary), not here. No interface labels inside ABE were glossed on this page except "Manage My Case" and the "Español" switch.
