# Translator notes: am-i-exempt.html (+ quiz.js `es` strings)

Files: `es/am-i-exempt.html`, `assets/js/quiz.js` (only the `es` block of STRINGS).
First-pass translation. Every checkbox and result card that encodes an eligibility criterion is listed below where a real wording choice was made. Rows marked **LEGAL** need the closest look.

General choices that apply across the page:
- **"work rules" → "requisitos laborales"** everywhere (glossary: one term site-wide). The English alternates "work rules" / "work requirements"; Spanish does not.
- **"meet the work rules" → "cumplir con los requisitos laborales"** in every card.
- **"ACA Adult" (singular, about the reader) → "estar en el grupo de adultos de la ACA"**, to avoid the gendered "un adulto / una adulta". Glossed "ACA Adults" once, at first use (Step 1).
- **Glosses at first use in DOM order** (style guide §4). Because quiz.js hides all cards but the ones that match, a reader may see a card (e.g. group-unsure, snap) whose Manage My Case / Case Summary labels were glossed only in a hidden card above. Consider repeating the glosses per card.
- **"(en inglés)" is placed inside the link text** (`<a>… (en inglés)</a>`), per the brief. Check other pages do the same.
- **"screener" → "herramienta de evaluación"** (glossary), not "prueba piloto".
